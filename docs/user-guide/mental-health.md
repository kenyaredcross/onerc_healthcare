# Mental Health consultation note: user guide

The note is **provisional**: the format is to be confirmed with the Mental Health Officer (D63, OI-7).

When you consult a patient in the **Mental Health** clinic (Start Consultation as usual), the consultation form shows a **Mental Health Assessment** with these sections:

1. **Assessment:**
   - visit type (new or follow-up) and who referred the patient;
   - onset and duration;
   - past psychiatric and medical history, and substance use;
   - personal and social history (including displacement and trauma).
2. **Mental State Examination:** appearance and behaviour, speech, mood, affect, thought form and content, perception, cognition, insight and judgement.
3. **Risk Assessment:**
   - **suicide risk** and **risk of harm to others**: None identified / Low / Moderate / High;
   - recent self-harm;
   - notes and a safety plan.
4. **Screening scores (optional):** PHQ-9 (0–27) and GAD-7 (0–21). The severity band is shown next to the score.
5. **Formulation and plan:** formulation, interventions (psychoeducation, counselling, medication, referral…) and the follow-up date.

**To complete the consultation** you must record both risk ratings and the mood. When either risk is **High**, or recent self-harm is ticked, a **safety plan** is also required. A red "High risk" line shows at the top of the form.

**Privacy:**
- Mental Health consultations are logged as sensitive every time they are opened.
- Midwives cannot see them.
- They are never sent to outreach tablets.

**Corrections** after completion: use **Add Addendum** on the consultation.

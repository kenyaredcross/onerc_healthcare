# Maternity (labour ward): user guide

**Who uses this:** the Midwife; also the Clinical Officer and Medical In-Charge. HIS Officers and Mental Health Officers can read the records.

## Admitting a mother

**Normal route.** Reception registers the visit, and triage routes her to **Maternity**. The visit then appears under **Maternity › Waiting for Admission**. Open it and press **Admit to Maternity**, then choose:
- the ward (her facility's Maternity Ward);
- a free bed, if beds are set up;
- the LMP, gravida and para.

**Active labour (fast path).** Open the patient (or register her first) and press **Admit in Labour**. The visit is opened for her and closed as "Admitted" at the same time.

**From a consultation.** A clinician can either:
- complete the consultation with outcome **Admitted**, after which the Midwife admits from that visit; or
- refer her internally to Maternity.

**After admission:**
- The admission gets an IP number such as `KLB-MAT-2026-00001`.
- The visit closes as Admitted, so it counts once.
- Fill in the admission record:
  - pregnancy details (EDD and gestation are worked out from the LMP);
  - medical, family and transfusion history;
  - previous pregnancies;
  - blood group, rhesus and syphilis;
  - the admission examination;
  - ICD-11 diagnoses.

## During labour
- **Start Labour** on the admission once she is in labour.
- **Open Partograph**, then press **Add hourly observation** each hour:
  - foetal heart rate, liquor, moulding;
  - cervical dilatation and descent;
  - contractions (number and duration);
  - oxytocin, drugs and fluids;
  - pulse, blood pressure, temperature and urine.

  Leave a box blank if it wasn't measured; 0 means measured as zero.
- **The chart** plots dilatation (×) and descent (○) against the **alert** (orange) and **action** (red) lines, with the foetal heart strip below. A banner warns when progress is slow or a reading is dangerous.
- **Rows can't be changed once saved.** To correct one, add a new row with a note.
- **Record Observations** on the admission records ward vital signs before and after delivery.

## Delivery
- **Record Delivery** opens the Summary of Labour:
  - mode of delivery (or born before arrival);
  - the three stages, blood loss, perineum;
  - the mother's observations;
  - **one row per baby**: outcome, sex, weight, Apgar at 1 and 5 minutes, measurements, resuscitation, vitamin K.

  For twins, use **Add Baby (twin)**.
- **Submit** the record. Each **live** baby becomes a patient straight away ("Baby" + the mother's last name, born today, linked to the mother), and the admission becomes **Delivered**. The record can't be cancelled afterwards; add an **Addendum** to correct it.

## Discharge
**Discharge** on the admission asks for:
- the outcome: discharged home, referred out, died (with the cause; Midwife, Clinical Officer or Medical In-Charge), or absconded;
- the mother's condition and notes.

Discharging frees the bed and makes the admission read-only.

## Reports
MOH 717 sections J (admissions, discharges, deaths, bed days) and K (deliveries by mode, live births by sex, stillbirths, maternal deaths) count from these records. They are provisional until the HIS Officers confirm the forms.
